**ExpressJS** is the minimal Node.js web framework that CASA is built on. CASA
offers "a shallow learning curve" precisely because it is composed of standard
Express [routers and middleware](/concepts/routers-and-middleware.md). You mount
a configured CASA app onto an Express app with [`mount()`](/concepts/mount.md),
and you extend it with ordinary Express middleware through
[Hooks](/concepts/hooks.md). CASA targets Express 4.
