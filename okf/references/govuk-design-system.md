The **GOV.UK Design System** provides styles, components, and patterns for
building UK government services that are accessible and consistent. CASA adopts
it so services get well-researched, accessible markup out of the box. Its
component macros are available in [templates](/concepts/templating.md) under
`govuk/components/*`, and CASA's own `casa/components/*` macros wrap them.
