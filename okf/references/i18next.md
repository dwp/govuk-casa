**i18next** is the library CASA uses under the hood for
[internationalisation](/concepts/i18n.md). It powers the `req.t()` and template
`t()` translation functions, language detection, namespaces, and string
interpolation. CASA loads dictionaries from JSON/YAML files organised by locale
and namespace.
