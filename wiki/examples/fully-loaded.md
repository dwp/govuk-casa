The fully loaded example demonstrates more of CASA's application surface than
the barebones example. It includes multiple page types, branching journey
definitions, hooks, global events, locales, and a sub-application.

# Use it for

* Studying conditional routing in a realistic Plan.
* Seeing how a sub-app can be integrated.
* Finding examples of page hooks and global hooks.
* Comparing form templates for common GOV.UK form patterns.
