The barebones example is the quickest reference for how a service wires CASA
together. It includes an application entrypoint, server, view templates, page
definitions, field definitions, a Plan, events, locales, and two example
plugins.

# Use it for

* Seeing the minimal shape of a CASA app.
* Copying the separation between `app.js`, `server.js`, definitions, views, and
  locales.
* Understanding how page definitions reference fields and templates.
