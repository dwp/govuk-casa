The DWP Frontend example demonstrates how a CASA app can use DWP-specific
frontend assets and page layouts while retaining CASA's journey, validation,
and templating patterns.

# Use it for

* Checking how extra frontend assets are compiled and served.
* Seeing custom layouts layered around CASA journey pages.
* Comparing locale-driven page content with the other examples.
